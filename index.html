Key features

An automatic memory system with almost no daily overhead.

On-device transcription

Designed around on-device transcription where possible, so private speech can become useful text without defaulting to the cloud.

Automatic journal

Your day can become a readable journal automatically, preserving tone, decisions, and what actually changed.

Memory timeline

Threads, people, places, moods, and projects become easier to revisit across weeks and years.

Reflective prompts

Focused summaries and questions help you spot recurring patterns without demanding constant check-ins.

Tasks and decisions

Extract possible follow-ups, open decisions, and commitments from the natural flow of your own speech.

Signal-aware capture

Overlap-aware transcription, smart segmentation, and VAD/silence-aware boundaries help turn messy audio into reliable searchable memory.

Privacy-first

Your life is not training data.

DayLog is built around a local-first model: transcription can happen on device, raw audio should be temporary by default, and cloud features should be explicit choices rather than hidden assumptions.

  • Local-first storage for sensitive transcripts
  • Clear controls for audio retention, export, deletion, and sync
  • On-device transcription where possible, with transparent fallback options
