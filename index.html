Key features

A calm system for remembering what matters.

Offline transcription

Designed for local-first capture, with on-device transcription where possible so your private audio does not need to leave your machine.

Automatic journal

Daily fragments can become readable entries that preserve tone, context, and what changed.

Memory timeline

Threads, people, places, moods, and projects become easier to revisit across weeks and years.

Reflective prompts

Gentle summaries and questions help you notice recurring patterns without turning DayLog into a chatbot.

Tasks and decisions

Extract possible follow-ups, open decisions, and commitments from the natural flow of your own speech.

Signal-aware capture

Overlap-aware transcription, smart segmentation, and VAD/silence-aware boundaries help turn messy audio into dependable day logs.

Privacy-first

Your life is not training data.

DayLog is being built around a local-first model: audio stays on your device by default, transcription can work offline, and cloud features should be explicit choices rather than hidden assumptions.

  • Default local storage for sensitive audio and transcripts
  • Clear controls for export, deletion, and future sync
  • On-device AI where possible, with transparent fallback options
